# ep 9 main wide. Every eye is on the main wide's side of the loop, inside the loop, or over a mark a body has
# stood on -- the two pillars on record are (11.9, 0.4) and (26.5, 13).
#   line 0  (snap) the main wide, held
#   line 1  cammove, no camsecs: the main wide pushes in and cranes down to a low 3/4 over the WHOLE line -- its
#           words, the gap and the next beat -- so it lands on the next cut
#   line 2  cammove + camsecs=4.0 on a 2.9 s line: it moves on from where line 1 left it (the carried end) ...
#   line 3  ... and, with no lens key, is CARRIED ON into this line until its 4 s are up (no cut)
#   line 4  camorbit round kaeli (slot 1), standing: headings 100 -> 260, the lens on her north side, the loop behind
#   line 5  camorbit 0,0 with `=2.2`: a WORLD-frame follow -- 4 u south of reva wherever she is on the loop, eye at

#   line 6  camshake=0.25,1.5 on the plain main wide: the handheld, alone
#   line 7  camtrack with `=2.6` (absolute eye height), the shake still on (sticky): azim 90 = her front = INSIDE
#           this loop. On this level floor `=2.6` and `2.6` frame alike; the half-pipe is where they part.
